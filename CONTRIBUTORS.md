CONTRIBUTORS
============

Symfony is the result of the work of many people who made the code better.

The full list of contributors is available online:

**https://symfony.com/contributors/code**

It is built from the commit history of the Symfony repositories and refreshed in
near real-time, so a contribution shows up shortly after it is merged.
Contributors who have a [Symfony Connect][1] account are linked to their
profile, where you can find more information about them and the other projects
they are involved in.

Want to appear there? Have a look at the [contributing documentation][2] to get
started; every merged pull request counts, from a typo fix to a new component.

[1]: https://connect.symfony.com/
[2]: https://symfony.com/doc/current/contributing/index.html
